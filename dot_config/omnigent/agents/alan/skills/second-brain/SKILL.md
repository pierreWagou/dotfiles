# second-brain — personal knowledge access

This skill provides access to your second-brain vault for personal knowledge,
voice profiles, and reference materials.

## Usage

When the agent needs to:
- Check your voice profile for writing style
- Access personal notes or reference materials
- Look up past decisions or context

Load this skill and read the relevant files from your vault.

## Vault Location

Your second-brain vault is typically at:
- `~/Documents/second-brain/` or
- `~/Projects/second-brain/` or
- Check your notes app (Notion, Obsidian, etc.)

## Voice Profile

For writing style guidance, read:
- `🪞 Personal/Voice Profile.md` in your vault

This contains your tone, style preferences, and writing conventions.
